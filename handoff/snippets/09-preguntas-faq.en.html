Where is Passage Amador located?

Passage is located on the Amador Causeway, Panama City, between the Canal, the Pacific and the city, minutes from Casco Antiguo.

What types of residences are offered?

Passage offers a range of residence layouts. This page presents a selection of five: Casia, Almendro, Cedro, Panamá and Palma Real, with options from 51 to 213 m². Request information to learn about other configurations and availability.

How can I find out about prices and availability?

Availability may vary by layout, tower and level. Request information and our team will share the options and prices currently available.

When is delivery expected?

Towers 3 and 4: first quarter of 2028. Towers 1 and 2: third quarter of 2029. Estimated dates, subject to developer confirmation.

What amenities does the project offer?

Every tower has a private rooftop with an infinity pool, solar deck, lounge, BBQ area and co-working space. The Clubhouse, shared by the whole community, brings together a spa with massage area, sauna and steam room, a pool with sun deck, BBQ and a lounge room for gatherings. The project also includes a padel court and a bike station; at the community level, a retail area, a park and tennis courts.

How can I visit Passage in person?

You can request a visit and meet our team at the Passage sales gallery on the Amador Causeway. Leave us your details or contact us directly via WhatsApp to arrange it.
